# Supplier Onboarding SOP

Owner: Procurement Operations. Applies to all new direct and indirect suppliers of Northwind Components GmbH (fictional company used for this demo).

## Required documents
Every new supplier must submit a signed Supplier Code of Conduct, a valid trade register extract, a VAT certificate and a completed Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ). Suppliers in manufacturing categories must additionally provide ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certificates or a written plan to obtain them within 18 months.

## Onboarding steps
1. The requesting buyer creates a supplier request in the CRM with category, country and expected annual spend.
2. Procurement Operations sends the SAQ link. The supplier has 10 working days to complete it.
3. The risk engine calculates an initial risk score (see Risk Scoring Methodology).
4. Suppliers with a score below 40 are approved automatically. Scores from 40 to 69 need approval by the Category Manager. Scores of 70 or above go to the Compliance Committee.
5. After approval, master data is created in the ERP and the supplier is set to status "Active".

## Service level
The target lead time from request to "Active" is 15 working days for low-risk suppliers and 30 working days for high-risk suppliers.

# ESG Data Collection from Suppliers

For the annual sustainability report (prepared in line with the European Sustainability Reporting Standards), Northwind collects selected data from its top suppliers.

## Scope
The top suppliers that together make up 80% of annual purchasing spend are in scope. The list is updated every January.

## Data points
- Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions (tCO2e) allocated to Northwind's purchases
- Share of renewable electricity used in production (%)
- Water withdrawal in water-stressed regions (m3)
- Number of recordable work accidents

## Timeline
The data request is sent on 15 January. Suppliers must respond by 31 March. Missing values are estimated with spend-based emission factors and flagged as "estimated" in the report.

## Data quality
Each value gets a quality flag: measured, calculated or estimated. The target is that at least 60% of Scope 1 and 2 values are measured or calculated.

# Supplier Audit Programme

## Audit types
- Desk audit: review of documents and SAQ evidence. Used for Medium risk suppliers.
- On-site audit: physical inspection by an approved third-party auditor. Mandatory for High risk suppliers and for all new suppliers in raw materials.
- Follow-up audit: verifies that a Corrective Action Plan was implemented.

## Frequency
High risk suppliers are audited on site every 12 months. Medium risk suppliers receive a desk audit every 24 months. Low risk suppliers are not audited routinely but can be sampled (5% per year).

## Findings
Findings are classified as critical, major or minor. A critical finding triggers the High-Risk Supplier Escalation process immediately. Major findings must be closed within 90 days, minor findings within 180 days.

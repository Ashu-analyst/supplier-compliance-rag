# Grievance Mechanism

Workers at suppliers, NGOs and other affected people can report concerns about human rights or environmental issues in the supply chain.

## Channels
Reports can be submitted through the web form, by email to the grievance inbox, or by phone through the multilingual hotline. Anonymous reports are accepted.

## Handling
- Acknowledgement of receipt within 7 days.
- Initial assessment by the Compliance Officer within 14 days.
- The reporter is informed about the outcome and any remedy.
- Retaliation against reporters is prohibited and is itself treated as a critical finding.

## Reporting
The number and type of grievances are reported quarterly to the Management Board and annually in the sustainability report.

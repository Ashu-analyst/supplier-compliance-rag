# Contract Clauses for Suppliers

Every framework agreement must contain the following clauses:

- **Code of Conduct clause**: the supplier commits to the Supplier Code of Conduct and to pass its requirements on to its own direct suppliers.
- **Audit clause**: Northwind or an appointed third party may audit the supplier with 10 working days' notice, or without notice if there is a reasonable suspicion of a serious violation.
- **Remedy clause**: the supplier must implement Corrective Action Plans agreed with Northwind.
- **Termination clause**: Northwind may terminate the agreement if serious violations are not remedied within the agreed period.

Standard templates are maintained by the Legal team. Deviations require approval from Legal and Compliance.

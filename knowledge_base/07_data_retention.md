# Data Retention and Access

## Retention periods
- Supplier master data: as long as the supplier is active, plus 10 years.
- SAQ answers and risk scores: 7 years.
- Escalation and grievance case records: 7 years.
- Personal data of grievance reporters: deleted 3 years after the case is closed, unless needed for legal proceedings.

## Access rights
Risk scores are visible to Procurement, Category Managers and Compliance. Grievance cases are visible only to the Compliance team. Board members receive aggregated reports only.

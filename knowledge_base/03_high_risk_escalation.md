# High-Risk Supplier Escalation

## Trigger
An escalation starts when a supplier reaches the High risk band (score 70 or above), when an audit finds a critical non-conformity, or when a grievance about forced labour, child labour or severe environmental damage is received.

## Steps
1. The Compliance Officer opens an escalation case within 2 working days.
2. The supplier receives a written request for a Corrective Action Plan (CAP). The supplier must submit the CAP within 20 working days.
3. The Compliance Committee reviews the CAP. It can accept it, ask for changes, or decide on a sourcing freeze.
4. During a sourcing freeze, no new purchase orders may be placed. Existing orders may be delivered.
5. If the CAP is not implemented within 6 months, the Committee decides on termination of the business relationship as a last resort.

## Documentation
All escalation steps, decisions and evidence must be documented in the case record. Records are kept for 7 years.

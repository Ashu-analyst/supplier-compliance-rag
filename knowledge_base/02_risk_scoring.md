# Risk Scoring Methodology

The supplier risk score runs from 0 (lowest risk) to 100 (highest risk). It is recalculated every quarter and whenever a supplier changes its SAQ answers.

## Components and weights
- Country risk (30%): based on an external governance and human-rights index for the supplier's country of production.
- Category risk (25%): inherent risk of the purchasing category. Raw materials, textiles and electronics assembly are rated high; office services and software are rated low.
- SAQ result (25%): inverse of the questionnaire score. Missing answers count as the worst answer.
- Adverse media (20%): number of verified negative news items in the last 24 months, capped at five.

## Risk bands
- Low: 0 to 39
- Medium: 40 to 69
- High: 70 to 100

## Overrides
A Compliance Officer may override a score by up to 15 points in either direction. Every override needs a written justification stored on the supplier record. Overrides expire after 12 months.
